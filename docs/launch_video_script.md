# Arcanis: Phase 1a Launch Video Script (v1.0, 2026-10-24)

**Purpose:** the launch video for the creation soft launch (Phase 1a). Creation only: no battles, duels, opponents, HP bars, minigames or "victory" shots (those are not live; the battle trailer `Arcanis_Promo_1080p.mp4` is kept for Phase 1b). Main goal: a stranger scrolling past stops in the first second, understands "type a spell, get a balanced card" by second 8, and wants to try to break it.

**Formats:** master is 9:16 vertical, 1080x1920, 30 fps, 30 seconds (YouTube Shorts, Reels, TikTok, WhatsApp status). Cut-downs at the end: 15 s vertical, and 16:9 1920x1080 for X and YouTube.

---

## Hard rules for whoever makes it (person or Claude)

1. **Every card on screen is a real spell forged on the live site.** No invented names or stats: invented numbers can break the stat caps and the Power Budget, and players will try the same prompt. Forge the four prompts below on the live site, then copy each spell's exact name, element, category, tier, damage or effect range, MP cost, Strain label and lore line into the scenes. Placeholders below are in `[BRACKETS]`.
2. **Will is never a number.** Will cost appears only as the live detail view shows it: the label "Strain" with the word Light, Moderate or Heavy (and its three-step bar, exactly as the live view draws it). Never the word Will with a number, percentage, meter or value.
3. **Match the live product.** The live card face shows the element art, the spell name and one lore line; tier shows in the frame ornament. Stats appear in the detail view, so show them as a detail strip beside or under the card, not printed on the card face. The live button is "Research Spell". Cast type on live spells is Instant.
4. **The art is element art, not AI images.** Never say or imply the AI drew the picture. The AI designs the spell; say that.
5. **No numbers we cannot back.** No "thousands of players", no "10,000 spells". Use the real Codex, as it is on the day.
6. Palette: black and gold, as in the battle trailer (same fonts: the Cinzel-style capitals and the Cormorant-style italic). No em dashes in any on-screen text.
7. **Burned-in captions on every beat.** Most people watch muted. All text sits inside the 9:16 safe area: nothing in the bottom 20% or the right 15% (platform buttons cover them) or the top 10%.

**Why this order works:** the first frame is already in motion with a challenge ("I tried to break this game") that makes people stay to see the answer; the answer at 4.5 s delivers the one thing no other AI toy promises (fair, balanced); the montage proves range and gives three more moments to laugh or be impressed; the Codex shows a world; "Battles are coming" turns a toy into a game you start early, which is exactly what we want the cohort to do (sign up and opt in before the battle launch).

## Cut-downs

- **15 s vertical:** 0.0 to 8.5 (the hook and the fair reveal), then jump to the logo and address (24.0 to 30.0, shortened to 6.5 s).
- **16:9 (X, YouTube):** same beats and timing. The prompt box sits left, the card right (as in the trailer's layout), captions in the upper third. X autoplays muted, so captions matter even more.
- **Series format (daily Shorts, marketing plan Section 4):** beats 0.0 to 8.5 only, with a new absurd prompt each day ("I tried to break this game, day 3"), always ending on the address. Player prompts are credited on screen with their mage name, with their permission.
