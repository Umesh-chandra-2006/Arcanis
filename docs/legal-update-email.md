Subject: Arcanis is now 18+, and our Terms and Privacy Policy have changed

Hello,

You have an Arcanis account, so here is what has changed.

Arcanis is now for people aged 18 and over. The next time you open Arcanis, you will be asked to confirm that you are 18 or older, to accept the new Terms, and to consent to your data being used as the new Privacy Policy describes. You cannot keep playing until you do. If you are under 18, please do not accept; write to us and we will delete your account.

The main privacy changes:

- No more device id for browsing. We now set a device identifier only when you sign in, and we use it only for the once-per-device free Spark and for rate limits. We no longer use it to count visits, and invite codes are no longer stored on your device.
- A notice about AI providers. The spell name and description you type are sent to Google or Groq to write your spell, and to OpenAI to check it for safety. Google may keep that text, learn from it and have people review it, so do not enter personal information.
- 12 month retention. Moderation excerpts, browser signature hashes and device identifiers are deleted after 12 months (a flagged item still waiting for review is kept until it has been reviewed).

Read them in full:

Terms: https://arcanis.world/terms
Privacy Policy: https://arcanis.world/privacy

To ask us to delete your account, write to support@arcanis.world.

Thank you for being one of the first mages,
Arcanis
